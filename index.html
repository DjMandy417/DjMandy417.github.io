"When I was in my 30s, I fell ill and needed emergency surgery. However, I had 3 children all under the age of 8. Ladies from my young mothers group helped me while I was incapacitated; when my husband had to work, one of the ladies even cared for my children overnight, so I could rest.

At the end of the week, I asked them, 'Why have you have done so much for me?' They said, 'We are the Body of Christ on Earth. How could we do any less?' I never forgot those words. Being a follower of Christ is not simply believing. It is acting on those beliefs. When others cannot, we can with Christ's guidance and help."

What does the club do?

We are a service club. We find out the needs of the people in our area and we try to meet them.
What services do we offer?

Our services include emergency meals, rides, running errands, check-ins, small emergency repairs, laundry, and more. If you have a need, just ask.
How can people get involved?

You can register on our schedule to be called when a need arises. Meetings will be listed on the website as soon as we have a schedule.
