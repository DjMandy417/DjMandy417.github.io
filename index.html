The Body of Christ Club

Church of St. Anne, Garwood, NJ

Who are we?

The Body of Christ Club is a new club started at the Church of St. Anne. Our mission is to serve the members in and out of our community. We want to help bear our neighbor's burdens. This website was made to make the process simple and accessible.

What do we offer?

Our services include emergency meals, rides, running errands, babysitting, dogsitting, check ins, small emergency repairs, laundry and more. The list is extensive and not exclusive! If you have a need, please ask!

Who can ask for help?

Anyone local to Garwood, Westfield, Cranford, and Clark (still reach out if you're further). Catholic or non-catholic. We are looking to service members of our community struggling from small or large life challenges .

How can I get involved?

You can fill out a form below, either to become a volunteer or requesting a need. Additionally, I welcome calls, texts, or emails, if that is your preference. Club meetings will be listed on the website as soon as we have a schedule.
